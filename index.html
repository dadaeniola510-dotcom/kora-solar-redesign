The story

Water shouldn't cost a childhood.

In rural communities, children spend hours each day collecting water instead of attending school. Much of it makes them sick.

We partner with local engineers to install solar-powered wells that serve whole villages for decades — no fuel, no fees, no waiting.

FAQ

Questions? Answered.

Where does my money go?

100% of donations go to drilling, solar pumps and local labour. Processing fees are covered by our partners.

Is my payment secure?

Yes. All payments are encrypted and processed by a PCI-compliant provider.

Will I get updates?

Every donor receives weekly progress updates with photos until the wells are complete.

Can I donate monthly?

Yes — monthly giving is available at checkout and can be cancelled anytime.
